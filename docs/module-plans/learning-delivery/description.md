# Learning Delivery

## Muc dich

Module `learning-delivery` so huu cach nguoi hoc di qua noi dung: bat dau hoc, luu lich su hoc tap, nop/lien ket bai lam, tinh dieu kien qua bai va mo khoa module tiep theo.

## Pham vi chuc nang

- Ghi nhan lich su hoc tap chi tiet.
- Theo doi trang thai hoan thanh noi dung hoc.
- Ap dung diem toi thieu de qua bai.
- Mo khoa module tiep theo khi dat diem yeu cau.
- Teacher thiet lap nguong diem qua bai cho lop.
- Admin cau hinh nguong diem mac dinh cua he thong.

## Vai tro

| Vai tro | Nhu cau |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Hoc theo module, lam bai dat diem X de mo khoa module tiep theo, xem lich su hoc. |
| Teacher | Thiet lap nguong diem qua bai cho lop va theo doi tien trinh hoc. |
| Admin | Cau hinh nguong diem mac dinh he thong. |

## Ngoai pham vi

- Noi dung hoc can content model moi sau khi module course bi go.
- Bai kiem tra va cham diem nam trong `assessments-grading`.
- Dashboard tong hop va diem yeu nam trong `learning-analytics`.
