# Classroom Management

## Muc dich

Module `classroom-management` so huu lop hoc co giao vien, membership, invite code, gan khoa hoc vao lop, phe duyet hoc vien va cac luong tuong tac Student - Teacher.

## Pham vi chuc nang

- Teacher tao lop va chon khoa hoc co san.
- Student tham gia lop qua code/moi.
- Teacher quan ly danh sach va phe duyet hoc vien.
- Admin quan ly danh sach toan bo lop.
- Giao vien quan ly tien do hoc sinh trong pham vi lop.
- Trao doi truc tiep Student - Teacher.

## Vai tro

| Vai tro | Nhu cau |
| --- | --- |
| Student | Tham gia lop qua code/moi va trao doi truc tiep voi giao vien. |
| Teacher | Tao lop, gan khoa hoc, quan ly danh sach, phe duyet hoc vien, xem tien do. |
| Admin | Quan ly danh sach toan bo lop va xu ly tranh chap neu co. |

## Ngoai pham vi

- Phan tich tien do chi tiet nam trong `learning-analytics`.
- Khoa hoc/lesson content nam trong `course-content`.
- Khieu nai/tranh chap cap he thong nam trong `platform-admin`.
